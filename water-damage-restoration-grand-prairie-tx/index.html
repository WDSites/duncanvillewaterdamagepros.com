Grand Prairie has a lot of creek and lake country, and that makes water calls here different from a plain pipe leak. Call the 24/7 emergency line, shut off the main valve and stay clear of water near electrical equipment. We dispatch from Duncanville and cover all four Grand Prairie ZIP codes.

About 36 percent of the city is floodplain

The city's floodplain plan says about 36 percent of Grand Prairie land is in the floodplain. It names Johnson, Mountain, Cottonwood and Fish Creeks, plus the West Fork Trinity River. That means the odds of outdoor water reaching a building are higher here than in many cities.

Flood water is treated as Category 3 under the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, meaning contaminated, and homeowners policies generally do not cover flood damage, so check your address and your policy.

Which county, which office

Grand Prairie spans Dallas, Tarrant and Ellis counties. For flood and drainage questions, reach the Stormwater office at 972-237-8251 or floodplain@gptx.org. For water dispatch, call 972-237-8400. Building Inspections at 972-237-8230 answers permit questions.

Dallas supplies about 85 percent of the city's water, so a pressure problem may start upstream of the city.
