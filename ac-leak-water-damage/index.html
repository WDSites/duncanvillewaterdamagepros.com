Yes, a leaking air conditioner can cause real water damage, and it is one of the more common indoor leaks in a Texas summer. Usually the condensate drain clogs, the pan fills and water spills into the ceiling, closet or attic floor. An HVAC technician fixes the unit and the drain. We dry the wet drywall, insulation and framing the water reached.

Why air conditioners leak in North Texas heat

A cooling coil pulls moisture out of the air, and that water drains away through a small condensate line. The National Weather Service Fort Worth office reports July and August highs in the 90s that often reach 100 F, and Gulf moisture keeps summer humidity high. A system that runs most of the day makes a lot of condensate, so a line coated with algae or dust backs up sooner.

Census counts show 14,227 housing units in Duncanville in 2020, and growth records from the Texas State Historical Association show many homes were built between 1960 and 1980. In many of those homes the air handler sits in a closet or the attic. A leak there can run for days before a stain shows on the ceiling below.

AC leak safety steps for the first hour

Turn the thermostat to off so the unit stops making more condensate. Switch off the breaker for the air handler, because water and a live unit do not mix. If a ceiling is bulging with water, stay out from under it and call the emergency line, since a loaded ceiling can drop without warning.

Do this before anyone arrives

  • Set the thermostat to off and cut power to the air handler at the breaker.
  • Move furniture and electronics out from under the wet ceiling.
  • Place buckets and towels under drips.
  • Photograph the stain, the pan and the drain line.
  • Call an HVAC company for the unit and the emergency line for the water.

How we dry a ceiling, closet or attic

The crew uses moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera to find how far the water traveled. Wet insulation comes out first because it holds water and dries slowly. Soaked drywall may need a cut section, and the rest dries with air movers and LGR dehumidifiers while we record readings in a drying log.

The air handler stays the technician's job, and we do not repair coils, pans or refrigerant lines. Often the best order is the HVAC repair first, so the leak stops, and then drying begins. If the leak has spread, our water mitigation work covers the drying plan from start to finish.

Cost factors for AC leak damage

Price depends on how long the leak ran, how much ceiling or insulation is wet, whether the attic floor or a closet slab is involved, and how many days of equipment the readings call for. The figure comes after the crew sees the space. Rebuild work such as new drywall and paint is scoped separately from drying, and our water damage repair page explains that step.

Insurance questions for AC leaks

Texas Department of Insurance guidance says to tell your agent or company as soon as you know about damage, since some policies set short notice windows for water. Coverage often depends on whether the leak was sudden or slow, so check your policy and keep photos. Never assume approval, and the TDI Consumer Help Line is 1-800-252-3439.

Mistakes to avoid and ways to prevent repeats

  • Running the unit while water still drips from it.
  • Painting over a ceiling stain before the framing above is dry.
  • Leaving wet insulation in place because the surface looks fine.
  • Skipping the yearly drain line cleaning that a technician can do.

Ask your HVAC company about a float switch, which shuts the unit off when the pan fills. It is a small part that can save a ceiling.
