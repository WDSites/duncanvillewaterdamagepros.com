Drying early prevents sagging drywall and keeps wet insulation from holding moisture for days.

How a roof leak damages a home here

The National Weather Service Fort Worth office reports 37.01 inches of normal annual rain for Dallas/Fort Worth, with spring the peak season for severe weather. Hail and damaging wind occur nearly every year somewhere in the region. A single storm can lift shingles, and rain then finds a path down.

Water follows framing, so the stain on your ceiling may sit far from the hole. That is why we check the whole area, not just the wet spot.

What you see and what it means

SignWhat it often means.

Roof leak safety before the rain stops

While the rain is still coming in

  • Move furniture and electronics out from under the leak.
  • Place buckets under active drips and keep people out from under bulging ceilings.
  • Stay out of the attic during a storm.
  • Turn off lights and fans on a wet ceiling circuit if it is safe to reach the panel.
  • Take photos of ceilings, walls and the roof damage if visible from the ground.

Our process inside the house

Crews use a moisture meter and thermal imaging camera to trace the wet path. Wet insulation is bagged and removed, and sagging drywall is cut out when it cannot dry. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers then dry framing and remaining materials, and daily drying logs record progress.

Large storm losses overlap with our storm water damage cleanup work, and the work follows ANSI/IICRC S500 as a published reference.

Roofer first or restoration first?

The order depends on the weather. If rain is still falling or more is forecast, the roof comes first because water keeps entering. If the sky is clear and the leak is stopped, drying can start at once.

If the roof is still open, call a roofer now. A temporary tarp keeps more water out. We cannot dry a house that keeps getting wetter. Many homeowners schedule both, since drying inside can start while the roofer works outside.

Ask the City of Duncanville Permit and Inspection Services whether your roof repair needs a permit. The city says repair work generally needs a permit and inspections.

Wet drywall that stays soft after a day usually cannot be saved. Ceiling drywall that is stained but firm can sometimes dry in place, and a moisture meter makes that call. Paint and texture are replaced after the framing and insulation are dry, which is part of the rebuild step.

Cost factors for roof leak cleanup

Cost depends on how many rooms the water reached, how much insulation and drywall must come out and how many days of drying equipment are needed. Ceiling height and attic access matter too. The price comes after the crew sees the damage.

Insurance for roof leaks

Texas Department of Insurance guidance says to give your insurer prompt notice. Some policies set shorter windows for water damage. Storm damage and long-term leaks are often handled differently, so check your policy. Photos of the ceiling and roof help.

The TDI Consumer Help Line is 1-800-252-3439. We never promise approval.

Roof leak mistakes to avoid

  • Waiting for the ceiling to dry on its own.
  • Painting over a stain before moisture is checked.
  • Climbing onto a wet roof.
  • Leaving wet insulation in the attic for days.
  • Skipping a spring roof check after hail season.

Roof leaks and Duncanville weather

Because severe weather peaks in spring, a quick look at your ceilings after each storm is a smart habit. Catching a small stain early keeps the repair smaller.

Storm days also bring heavy demand for roofers, so call yours early.

Not every leak comes from hail. Flashing around vents, worn shingles and wind lifted edges all let water in during ordinary rain. A roofer can say which one failed. We focus on what is wet, how far it spread and how long drying will take.
