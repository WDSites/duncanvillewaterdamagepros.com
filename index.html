Drying is measured, not guessed. Crews log moisture readings each visit and keep equipment running until the wet materials read dry, which is how a job can take three days or a week and still be done correctly.

Most restoration firms follow the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage and S520 for mold. You can ask any company, including us, whether its crews work to those published standards.

Local conditions

Why Water Damage Happens in Duncanville Homes

Duncanville grew fast. The city counted 3,774 residents in 1960 and 27,781 in 1980, so many homes here are now 45 to 65 years old. Older supply lines, water heaters and shower valves fail with age, which makes slow leaks inside walls a common story, and a good reason to check water damage repair options before a stain spreads.

Weather adds its own pressure. The National Weather Service says Dallas and Fort Worth average 29 freezes a season, and severe thunderstorms peak in spring with hail and damaging winds. A thaw can expose a split pipe, and a hail-damaged roof can drip into a ceiling days after the storm.

Creek water is part of the picture too. In 2023 the city responded to a sewer overflow near Harrington Park, at Tenmile Creek and Stewart Branch. That is why the city tells residents to avoid contact with affected water, and why flood damage restoration treats outside water as contaminated until proven otherwise.

Where you live inside the city matters less than what the water touched. A kitchen supply leak near Main Street and a roof leak near Armstrong Park both need the same first steps: stop the source, protect the room and call the line. If your home sits across the line in Oak Cliff, call and dispatch will tell you whether we can reach the address.

Know before you call

How Water Category Changes the Plan

Restoration crews classify water by what it carried before it reached your floor. The category sets the safety steps, the materials that can be saved and how long the job runs.

CategoryTypical sourceWhat it means for the job.
Category 1Broken supply line, tap overflow, appliance supply leakClean at first. Fast drying can save drywall, carpet and subfloor.
Category 2Washing machine drain, dishwasher discharge, toilet overflow without solidsContains contaminants. Porous materials such as carpet pad are often removed.
Category 3Sewage backup, creek or storm floodingGrossly contaminated. Affected porous materials come out and surfaces are sanitized.

Water that sits can move up a category. Time is the reason to call early.

Cost and claims

What Decides the Price and What to Tell Your Insurer

Nobody can quote a fair number over the phone for a job they have not seen. Five things move the price: the water category, the square footage that got wet, the materials involved, how many drying days and which equipment the structure needs, and how much rebuilding follows. The scope and the price are explained before work starts, and you can ask questions at every step.

Insurance and Documentation

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered by homeowners policies. Gradual leaks and neglect often are not, and flood damage usually needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy and ask your insurer.

Photos, moisture readings and a written scope help any claim. We document the loss, but your insurer decides coverage, and we never promise that a claim will be approved.

While you wait

What to Do Before the Crew Arrives

Safe first steps

  • Stop the source if you can reach it, starting with the main water shutoff valve.
  • Turn off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and safe to reach. If water touches outlets or the panel, stay out and call the utility or 911 first.
  • Lift valuables, rugs and electronics out of the water.
  • Keep people and pets away from sewage or floodwater.
  • Take photos and video of the damage before anything is moved.
  • Call the line and tell dispatch what you see.
