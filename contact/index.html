What to have ready when you call

Details that help dispatch

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • Where the water came from and whether it is stopped.
  • Which rooms are wet and roughly how deep the water is.
  • Whether the water looks clean or dirty.
  • Whether power is on in the wet areas.
  • Your insurer, if you plan to file a claim.

Not sure which service you need? The services page explains each one. Ask any company, including us, whether its crews work to the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage.
