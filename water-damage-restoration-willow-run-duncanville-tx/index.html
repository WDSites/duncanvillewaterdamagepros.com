When rain is the cause

The National Weather Service Fort Worth office reports normal annual precipitation of 37.01 inches for Dallas Fort Worth, with spring and fall typically the wettest seasons. Severe weather peaks in spring, and hail and damaging wind occur nearly every year somewhere in the region.

That is regional climate, not a record of damage in Willow Run. It does explain why roof flashing, window seals and gutters matter. Water that gets past them shows up as a ceiling stain, a wet wall base or damp insulation.

First steps when water comes in

  • Move furniture and belongings away from the wet area if it is safe.
  • Catch drips with a bucket and photograph the stain.
  • Keep out of rooms where water is near outlets or light fixtures.
  • Do not climb onto a wet roof. A roofer handles the roof.
  • Call and describe where the water entered.

It names Ten Mile Creek, Horne Branch and Mauk Branch and does not tie any event to a named neighborhood. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified for Willow Run.

Call the City Floodplain Administrator at (972) 707-3856 to ask about a specific address.

Safety

Do not wade into water that may be electrically live or contaminated. Water from outdoors can carry bacteria and should be treated as unsafe. Shut off the main water valve only if it is accessible and dry, and call the electric utility or 911 for any immediate danger.

What crews do

The work begins with a moisture check of ceilings, walls and floors, then removal of standing water and of soaked insulation or carpet that cannot dry in place. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the rest. See roof leak cleanup, storm water damage cleanup and structural drying for the detail.

If the water was a flood from outdoors rather than a leak, read the flood damage restoration page.

Rain damage claims and cost

Take photos, note when the water started and keep damaged items until your insurer says otherwise. Coverage depends on the cause and your policy, and homeowners policies generally do not cover flood, according to the Texas Department of Insurance. Cost depends on the area wet, the materials, drying days and what is removed, and a number comes after the space is seen.
