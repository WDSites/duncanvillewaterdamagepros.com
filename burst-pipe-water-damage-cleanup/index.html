Why pipes burst in Duncanville homes

Freezing gets the attention, but pipes also split from age, high water pressure and worn fittings. Texas State Historical Association counts show the city grew from 3,774 people in 1960 to 27,781 in 1980, so many homes are now 45 to 65 years old. Old supply lines and connectors wear out whatever the weather does.

A split fitting can empty a lot of water in minutes. A pinhole inside a wall can drip for weeks before a stain shows. Knowing where your shutoff valve sits is the best tie-back to all of this.

Burst pipe safety in the first hour

Do this before the crew arrives

  • Close the main water shutoff by turning it clockwise.
  • Open a low faucet to drain what is left in the lines.
  • Cut power to wet rooms only if the panel is dry and safe to reach.
  • Call the electric utility or 911 first if water is near the panel or outlets.
  • Move small items and photograph the damage before anything is changed.
  • Call the 24/7 emergency line and describe what you see.

If you cannot find or close your shutoff, the City of Duncanville utility page lists an after-hours water emergency line at (972) 780-4959. Call a plumber too, because we do not repair pipes.

How the crew removes the water and dries the walls

Crews confirm the source is stopped and check for electrical risk. Wet vacuums and extraction units pull standing water, and a submersible pump handles deeper water. Then the drying phase starts, and it works hand in hand with water mitigation to limit what must be torn out.

Stop and survey

Confirm the shutoff and map wet areas with meters and a thermal camera.

Extract

Remove standing water from floors and carpet.

Open and dry

Cut small openings where needed, then set air movers and LGR dehumidifiers.

Log and verify

Record daily moisture readings until the materials are dry.

Our approach follows the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration. You can ask any company whether its crews work to it. Standing water first is covered in more detail on our emergency water removal page.

What drives the cost of a burst pipe job

Cost depends on how long the water ran, how many rooms it reached, what the floors and walls are made of and how many days of drying equipment the structure needs. Removal of ruined drywall or insulation adds scope. We give no number over the phone. The figure comes after the crew sees the space and explains the plan.

Insurance after a burst pipe

Texas Department of Insurance guidance says to notify your agent or company as soon as you know about damage. Deadlines vary by policy, and some are shorter for water damage. A sudden burst is treated differently from a slow leak, and homeowners policies generally do not cover flood. Check your policy and keep your photos.

The TDI Consumer Help Line is 1-800-252-3439. We never promise approval of a claim.

Mistakes to avoid after a burst

  • Waiting to see if it dries by itself while walls stay wet.
  • Running a household fan and calling it done.
  • Walking through water near outlets or a wet electrical panel.
  • Tearing out drywall before photographing it.
  • Skipping the plumber and only drying, which lets the pipe fail again.
Local note

Duncanville and the 2021 winter storm

The National Weather Service Fort Worth office reports an average of 29 freezes a season for the region, and most cause no harm. The 2021 winter storm was different. FEMA declared DR-4586 that year and designated Dallas County for Individual Assistance.

The City of DeSoto says its crews handled about 400 burst pipe and shutoff calls during that storm. The lesson for you is simple. Find your shutoff valve before cold weather arrives, not while water is moving.
