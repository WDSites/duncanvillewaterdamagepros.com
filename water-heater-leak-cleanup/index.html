If your water heater is leaking, turn off its power or gas and close the cold-water supply valve to it. Then call the 24/7 emergency line. We extract the water and dry floors and walls. A plumber replaces or repairs the heater.

How water heater leaks happen

Tanks fail slowly as metal wears and fittings loosen. Common leak points are the tank itself, the relief valve discharge, and the supply connectors. A tank leak is often the heater's end of life, and a plumber replaces the unit.

Census counts show 14,227 housing units in Duncanville in 2020, and many are older homes. Older water heaters and closet or garage installations are common places to check after any leak.

Water heater safety steps before cleanup

Before the crew arrives

  • Turn off electric power or gas to the heater.
  • Close the cold-water supply valve to the tank.
  • Do not touch the tank or standing water if outlets are wet.
  • Keep children and pets out of hot water.
  • Call the emergency line and a plumber.

If you cannot stop the flow, the City of Duncanville utility page lists an after-hours water emergency line. Water supplied by the city is treated Dallas water, so a clean supply line leak is usually Category 1 at first.

How we dry a water heater leak

Crews extract standing water with wet vacuums and extraction units. Moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera check baseboards, drywall and flooring. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers then dry the area, with daily logs.

Our work follows ANSI/IICRC S500 as a published reference. Wet drywall near the heater may need a flood cut, and our appliance leak page covers the hidden subfloor moisture that other machines cause.

Where the heater sits changes the job. In a closet, water runs into hallway flooring and wall bases. In a garage, it may stay on concrete and dry faster. In an attic, it can drop through the ceiling below, which brings insulation and drywall into the plan.

Cost factors for heater leak cleanup

Cost depends on how long the leak ran, what the floor is made of and whether walls need to open. Carpet, wood and tile dry differently. Drying days and equipment drive the total, and the number comes after an on-site look.

Insurance for heater leaks

Texas Department of Insurance guidance says to tell your insurer as soon as you know of damage. Sudden tank failure and slow seepage are treated differently. Check your policy and keep photos of the heater label and the damage.

The TDI Consumer Help Line is 1-800-252-3439. We never promise approval.

Water heater mistakes and prevention

  • Ignoring a small puddle near the base.
  • Storing boxes or carpet against the heater.
  • Leaving a drain pan empty and unchecked.
  • Replacing the heater but never drying the wall behind it.
  • Waiting on a weeping relief valve.
Local note

Water heaters and Duncanville seasons

The National Weather Service says summer highs often reach 100 F, and a hot garage or attic stresses heaters. A yearly check of the tank and drain pan helps.

A freeze can also stress exposed lines near a heater in an unheated space.

Hot water leaks need care. A burst tank can release many gallons quickly, and the water may be hot enough to injure. Keep clear until it cools. Once the plumber has shut off and removed the old unit, we dry the pad area, flooring and wall bases before a new heater goes in.
